Base surface container: `--surface` fill, hairline `--line` border, 14px radius. Used for stat tiles, account rows, envelope groups.

```jsx
<Card>Regular content</Card>
<Card elevated padding="30px 32px">Hero / banner content</Card>
```

Only hero/banner cards (e.g. "Safe to spend", net-worth banner) use `elevated` — most cards are flat with a hairline border.
